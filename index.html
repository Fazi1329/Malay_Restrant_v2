Our Provenance & Story

From Bambalapitiya to Dehiwala's Grand Dining Hub

The culinary story of Malay Fast Foods (Pvt) Ltd began in 1981 with the iconic Rasa Sayang restaurant in Bambalapitiya, which introduced discerning Colombo diners to authentic Sri Lankan Malay recipes passed down through generations.

In 1994, building upon this proud heritage, Malay Restaurant opened its doors at 115 Hill Street in Dehiwala. Over the past three decades, it has blossomed into an island-wide landmark celebrated for rich, slow-simmered beef curries, authentic babath (tripe) stew, spleen curries, banana-leaf lamprais, and vibrant hand-crafted mojitos.

1981

The Rasa Sayang Foundation

Origins in Bambalapitiya establishing the standard for authentic Sri Lankan Malay offal curries, satays, and sambols.

1994

Malay Restaurant Flagship at 115 Hill Street

Opening of the flagship restaurant in Dehiwala, expanding into an air-conditioned family haven with multi-cuisine dining.

A 6-Brand Culinary Epicenter
