The Nelum Tradition

Wholesome Village Cooking on Hill Street

Established at 93 Hill Street, Nelum - The Ceylon Kitchen is celebrated as Dehiwala's beloved authentic eatery ("buth kadey") where diners experience genuine island home cooking.

Starting at 7:00 AM with our famous Big Breakfast Combos, string hoppers with coconut kiri hodi, and warm pol rotti with lunumiris, moving into fragrant banana-leaf wrapped lunchtime rice and curry with fresh Linna, Barramundi, prawns, and village vegetables.
