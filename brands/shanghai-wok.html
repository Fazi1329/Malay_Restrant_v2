The Craft of Shanghai Wok

The Perfect Balance of Heat, Smoke & Flavor

Operating out of 97 Hill Street alongside Café Asiana, Shanghai Wok was launched to bring authentic Sri Lankan-Chinese fusion to Dehiwala. Our master chefs utilize round carbon-steel woks over high-pressure flame burners to flash-fry fresh seafood and meats, locking in juices and crisp textures.

Whether you crave classic Chicken Fried Rice, fiery Black Pepper Beef, or fresh Lagoon Prawns tossed with spring onions and garlic, every order is made fresh to order.
