The Café Asiana Experience

Where Indian Flavours Meet Contemporary Asian Dining

Café Asiana was created by Malay Fast Foods (Pvt) Ltd to offer Dehiwala a relaxed, modern culinary setting with an expansive menu spanning authentic Hyderabadi Dum Biryanis, sizzling starters, traditional clay-oven tandoori breads, rich gravies, and Asian stir-fries.

Our signature Chicken Matka Dum Biryani Set includes a half tandoori chicken, boiled & fried egg, rich chicken gravy, mint chutney, and cool raita—celebrated by locals as one of the best value-for-money feasts in the city.
